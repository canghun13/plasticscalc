Cavity Balance and Shot Weight Measurement Guide

Collect cavity-numbered short shots and repeatability samples without losing the measurement context.

Measurement setup

Confirm scale resolution against the expected spread, use the same conditioning interval, separate each cavity, and record consecutive cycles in production order. Tare containers and document whether the runner is included.

Balance versus repeatability

Cavity balance compares positions within a shot. Shot-weight repeatability compares cycles over time. A stable overall shot can still hide a repeatable weak cavity; a balanced single shot can still come from an unstable check ring or feed condition.

Decision criteria

Use weight as a sensitive screen, then review critical dimensions, appearance, functional requirements and the approved validation protocol. The max-to-min percentage and CV are evidence summaries, not release specifications.
