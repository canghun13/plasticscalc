### 2. Cooling Circuit Balance result cards

- Root: `mountBalance` creates light `.panel` cards inside a dark `.result` container; the light card gets a background but no foreground, so body paragraphs inherit the parent's pale text. Numbers, method and comparison flags themselves were correct.
- Fix only `.calculator[data-calculator="cooling-circuit-balance"] .calc-result-panel>.panel` foreground to`var(--ink)`. New card paragraph contrast **14.7739:1**. Outer dark result caption/median and all other result panels unchanged.

### 3. Film keyboard focus

- Expanded actual keyboard Tab/Shift+Tab checks found five Film submit buttons with **3.4979:1** while focus-visible. Default state was healthy, so a default-only check would miss this.
- Root: the existing Film inline template sets white text on`:focus-visible` but only the global`:hover` rule darkens the background. With keyboard focus and no pointer hover, the orange background remains.
- Live Film Tensile reproduction: white text/orange background, focus-visible true, outline auto. Scoped shared rule gives only Film submit`:focus-visible` the existing dark hover background/border; text stays white and native outline stays auto. New focus contrast **5.4886:1**. No Film HTML/generator change or loss of keyboard focus indicator.

Primary basis: [W3C WCAG2.2 contrast-minimum explanation](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html). Normal text requires4.5:1; the large-text threshold is not applicable to12px labels, and keyboard-focus text is included. This is a measured contrast correction, not a claim of whole-site WCAG compliance. No legal certification or unrelated redesign.
