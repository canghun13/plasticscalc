Shot Size Calculator

Calculate molded injection shot weight from cavity parts and the cold runner or sprue, with a separate planning allowance. This practical tool is an engineering estimate—validate decisions against machine, material, mold, and process data.

Decision chain

From estimate to the next check

  1. Complete shotPart mass × cavities plus runner or sprue
  2. Planning allowanceSeparate estimate, not ejected mass or measured screw cushion
  3. Volume basisMelt density when the next check is volumetric
  4. Machine checkDocumented screw, stroke, and injection-unit rating

Formula and method

Molded shot mass = part mass × active cavities + ejected cold runner/sprue mass. Planning mass = molded shot mass × (1 + allowance ÷ 100).

Inputs and units

Enter measured or specified values in the displayed units. Keep units consistent; results are rounded for practical planning. This first release uses the displayed engineering units rather than a unit-switcher.

Worked example

45 g × 4 cavities + 12 g runner = 192 g molded shot mass. A 10% illustrative allowance adds 19.2 g, giving 211.2 g planning mass.

Build a complete shot record

Use a weighed finished part or validated CAD mass, the actual cavity count, and a weighed cold runner or sprue. Weigh runner and sprue together once. Use the same part mass only for identical active cavities. A planning allowance is optional; the default 10% illustrates the arithmetic and is not a recommended setting.

Compare like with like at the injection unit

Take the machine rating, screw, and material basis from the machine data sheet. A quoted PS shot-weight rating is not a material-neutral barrel capacity; use shot volume when a volumetric comparison is needed.

Sanity check before trials

If no cold feed is ejected, enter 0 g runner/sprue and the molded shot equals the total part mass. Use molded shot mass for per-cycle material accounting. Confirm actual screw cushion separately on the machine; it is residual melt, not material ejected each cycle.

Interpretation, assumptions, and limitations

This tool provides an estimate for planning and education. It does not replace machine maker limits, material data sheets, mold trials, cavity-pressure data, process validation, or qualified engineering review.

FAQ

Does a hot runner make runner mass zero?

Only enter zero cold feed if none is ejected. Some hot-runner molds still have a cold sub-runner or sprue. Check the actual mold and verify screw cushion separately.
